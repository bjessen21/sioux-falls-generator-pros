Who We Are

A Sioux Falls Team That Only Does Standby Generators

We started Sioux Falls Generator Pros because too many homeowners here find out their backup power plan doesn't work in the middle of a blizzard — when it's too late to fix. That happens when a generator gets installed as an afterthought instead of by someone who sizes the load correctly, sites the unit properly, and wires the transfer switch to code.

We keep our focus narrow on purpose: standby generator installation, transfer switches, and the maintenance that keeps a system ready. That focus means fewer surprises, straighter answers, and a system that actually starts the next time the power goes out.

Why Sioux Falls Homes Need Backup Power

Winter Storms and Summer Thunderstorms Both Take Down Power

Sioux Falls sees ice storms, blizzards, and high winds in the winter, and severe thunderstorms in the summer — any of which can knock out power for hours or days. Losing power in a South Dakota winter isn't just an inconvenience; it can mean frozen pipes and an unsafe home in a matter of hours. A properly sized standby generator starts automatically within seconds, before your home even has a chance to lose heat.

  • Automatic startup — no manual setup or extension cords
  • Keeps heat, refrigeration, and sump pumps running
  • Sized for your home's actual electrical load, not a guess
  • Connects to your existing natural gas or propane line

Common Questions

Generator Installation FAQ

How long does whole-home generator installation take?

Most installs are completed in 1-2 days on-site once permits are approved. From your first call to a finished, inspected install, budget 2-4 weeks total depending on permit turnaround and equipment availability.

Do I need a permit to install a standby generator in Sioux Falls, SD?

Yes — standby generators typically require an electrical permit and inspection through the City of Sioux Falls or Minnehaha County, depending on your address. We handle the permitting process as part of every installation.

What size generator do I need for my home?

It depends on your square footage, HVAC equipment, and whether you want whole-house or essential-circuits-only coverage. We calculate your home's actual electrical load during the free on-site assessment rather than guessing off square footage alone.

Natural gas or propane — which is better for a Sioux Falls home?

Most Sioux Falls neighborhoods have natural gas access, which is the simpler and lower-maintenance option since it doesn't require refilling a tank. Propane is used for properties without a natural gas line. We confirm what's available at your address during the assessment.

How often does a standby generator need maintenance?

We recommend an annual service visit (oil, filter, battery, and a load test) plus the unit's built-in weekly self-test. In South Dakota's cold, batteries are the most common wear item.

Can you install a generator on an existing older home?

Yes — older electrical panels sometimes need an upgrade to safely accept a transfer switch, which we'll flag during the on-site assessment and include in your quote if needed.

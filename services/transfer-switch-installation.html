Transfer Switch Installation in Sioux Falls, SD

The part that makes standby power safe, legal, and automatic.

Required for Safety

Why a Transfer Switch Matters

An automatic transfer switch is what makes standby power safe and legal — it isolates your home's electrical panel from the grid the instant your generator kicks on, protecting utility line workers and preventing damage to your generator. Without one, a generator can "back-feed" power onto grid lines, which is dangerous and against code.

We also install manual transfer switches for homeowners pairing a portable generator with select circuits instead of a full standby system.

  • Automatic transfer switches for whole-home standby systems
  • Manual transfer switches for portable generator setups
  • Panel compatibility assessment and upgrades if needed
  • Code-compliant wiring and inspection

Automatic vs. Manual

Which Transfer Switch Do You Need?

Automatic Transfer Switch

Pairs with a whole-home standby generator. Detects an outage and switches your home to generator power within seconds — no one has to be home to flip anything.

Manual Transfer Switch

Pairs with a portable generator. You select which circuits get power and start the transfer by hand — a lower-cost option for essential-circuits-only backup.

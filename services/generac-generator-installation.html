Generac Generator Installation in Sioux Falls, SD

The most widely installed standby brand in the country, sized and sited specifically for South Dakota winters.

Why Generac

A Brand With Parts and Techs on the Ground Here

We install a lot of Generac because the Guardian Series covers most Sioux Falls homes well and, just as important, the parts and service network behind it is strong in this area. That matters most on the day your generator actually needs a battery, a filter, or a control board — you're not waiting weeks on a special order from a brand with no local footprint.

Every Generac install starts the same way regardless of model: an on-site load calculation, a site walk for pad placement and clearances, and a written quote before anything gets ordered. From there we handle the concrete pad, gas line coordination, electrical hookup, and the City of Sioux Falls or Minnehaha County permitting and inspection.

  • Free on-site load calculation, sized to your actual panel and home
  • Guardian Series units from 10kW essential-circuit setups up to 24kW+ whole-home coverage
  • Concrete pad placement and clearance siting built for Sioux Falls' setback and snow-load considerations
  • Mobile Link Wi-Fi monitoring setup on supported units
  • City/county permitting and final inspection handled for you

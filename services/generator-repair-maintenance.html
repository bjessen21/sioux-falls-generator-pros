Keep It Running

A Generator Only Helps If It Starts

Standby generators sit outside year-round through South Dakota heat, snow, and everything in between — which means batteries, air filters, and oil need regular attention or the system won't start when you actually need it. We service existing units regardless of who installed them, and we prioritize active outage calls.

  • Annual service: oil, filter, battery, and a full load test
  • Repair for Generac, Kohler, Briggs & Stratton, and other brands
  • Battery replacement — the most common winter failure point
  • Priority response for active power-outage calls

Signs to Watch For

When to Call for Generator Repair

Won't Start During an Outage

If your generator failed to kick in the last time you lost power, don't wait for the next outage to find out why.

Weekly Self-Test Errors

Most standby units run a short self-test weekly — an error code or failed test is an early warning sign worth checking.

No Maintenance in Over a Year

Skipped annual service is the most common reason a generator fails right when it's needed most.

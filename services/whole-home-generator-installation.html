Whole-Home Generator Installation in Sioux Falls, SD

Generac, Kohler, and Briggs & Stratton systems — sized to your home, not a guess.

New Installation

Automatic Backup Power, Done Right the First Time

A standby generator installs permanently outside your home, connects directly to your existing propane or natural gas line, and starts automatically within seconds of a power outage — no manual setup, no running extension cords, no fumbling in the dark.

We handle the full process: load calculation to size the unit correctly, concrete pad placement, gas line coordination, electrical hookup, permitting, and the final utility/inspection sign-off.

  • Free on-site load calculation and site assessment
  • Concrete pad placement and proper clearance siting
  • Gas line coordination (natural gas or propane)
  • Electrical hookup and automatic transfer switch wiring
  • City/county permitting and final inspection
